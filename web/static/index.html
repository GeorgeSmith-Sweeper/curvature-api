About Curvature

Curvature scores represent the "twistiness" of a road based on turn radius and frequency.

  • 300-600: Pleasant, flowing roads
  • 600-1000: Fun, moderately twisty
  • 1000+: Very curvy, technical roads
  • 2000+: Extremely twisty!
